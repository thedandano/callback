# React

**Situation:** Fielding's sales and support teams requested new client-reporting metrics through email, and each of the 12 recurring requests took a full sprint of engineering time to turn into a spreadsheet.

**Behavior:** Did cross-functional work with sales and support to scope a self-serve dashboard, then built it in React so non-engineers could add a metric without a ticket.

**Impact:** The 12 recurring spreadsheet requests became a self-serve dashboard, freeing roughly a sprint of engineering time a month.
